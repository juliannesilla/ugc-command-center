What This MeansFor My Deliverable

Translated from SOW into shoot-ready intent.

Your Goal

Make a 55-sec talking-head that sells the interview-brain-freeze pain point — and lands ParakeetAI as the natural fix without sounding like an ad.

Audience

Software engineers, AI/ML candidates, and adjacent operators actively interviewing — skeptical of obvious AI-promo content.

Key Focus

The 'my brain deletes my career' moment. Marketing-ops credibility layer beneath a software/interview story.

Do's

  • ▸Open on the interview-blank moment, not on the product
  • ▸Use marketing-ops background as credibility, not lead-with
  • ▸Show brief screen of Parakeet panel during a mock answer
  • ▸End with a single clean CTA — link in bio, no hard-sell

Don'ts

  • ▸No 'cheating the interview' or 'guaranteed offer' framing
  • ▸No pretending to be a full-stack engineer
  • ▸No hardship-reveal (laid-off detail stays off-camera)

Tip

Cite paragraph numbers from the SOW in every requirement card — that's how this stays definitive instead of "likely."
